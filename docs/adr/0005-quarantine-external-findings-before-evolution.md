# Quarantine external findings before self-evolution

External-tool findings enter Plastic Promise as immutable Evolution Evidence and may be projected into cited knowledge, but they cannot directly become canonical memory, active knowledge, or an Evolution Rule. A candidate rule requires source-independent corroboration, a reproducible regression fixture, shadow and canary evidence, explicit scope, and a reversible CAS promotion; knowledge-memory-knowledge cycles and repeated internal citations never count as independent support.

## Consequences

DeepSec is a required review sensor, not an authority. Its Shield output keeps scanner version, scan identity, rule ID, source revision/hash, file/line evidence, and dismissal state. DeepSec Spear is excluded from automated review, and no evolved rule may modify scanner configuration or production policy without a separate governed promotion.
